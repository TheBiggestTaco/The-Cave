✦ Big Bang Laboratory

128 points · Energy 50% · Attraction 30% · Bonding on · Collisions on · Seed CAVE-001

Experiment 005 mutates each point’s bond capacity (roughly 2–8) in response to crowding and accumulated strain; overloaded connections are shed and can regrow after cooldown. Toggle variable connectivity off to compare with Experiment 004. Experiment 004 adds sustained-strain bond fatigue to the local-only growth of Experiment 003. Turn fatigue off to reproduce Experiment 003. Toggle growth and reset to compare against the original CAVE-001 control. Crowded particles grow, recolor, and change bond rest lengths; sparse particles recover.

Click/tap the simulation to apply a radial impulse. Interactions are logged and replayable from the seed.

OBSERVATORY

The universe begins at its first tick.

Particles attract, repel at short distances, and form/break spring bonds. Stable connected structures emerge if the rules permit them.

Toy physics, not a cosmological model. Fixed-step seeded simulation; reset repeats the same initial conditions.

THE FIRST ROOM

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or controller. Interact using E, Space, or controller A.

Activate the glowing points to form a room. Find the doorway to discover another chamber.

Rule Map

Points connect into lines. Closing a loop forms a room. Opening a room creates a corridor. Crossing a corridor reveals another chamber.

Future generations may change these rules, while preserving this playable ancestor.
