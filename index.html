All physics toggles now change the running world without resetting particles, bonds or history. Reset / Replay alone starts a new run. Optional local adaptation lets mutated, crowded particles respond differently to expansion and containment. Optional center-outward force recreates the outward bias explored in Experiment 002; it can encourage splitting but does not guarantee division. The force is disabled by default and resets the seed when toggled. Experiment 009 measures mass-weighted angular momentum about the center of mass, direction reversals, and individual orbital contributions without changing physics. Positive angular momentum means clockwise on screen. Experiment 008 gives closed loops meaningful interiors: nearby particles can enter, interior particles experience a gentle inward retention force, and the perimeter resists crossing. Particle tails record position, color, size, and open/closed connection state. Experiment 007 traces the mass-weighted center, records departures and compares their outward direction with later cluster displacement. Toggle momentum conservation to test whether loop forces cause artificial drift; reset uses the same seed. Experiment 006 lets individual bonds transition between open lines and closed circular loops. Proximity and low relative speed encourage closure; stretching reopens them. Closed loops have a different spring response and repel points near their perimeter. Experiment 005 mutates each point’s bond capacity (roughly 2–8) in response to crowding and accumulated strain; overloaded connections are shed and can regrow after cooldown. Toggle variable connectivity off to compare with Experiment 004. Experiment 004 adds sustained-strain bond fatigue to the local-only growth of Experiment 003. Turn fatigue off to reproduce Experiment 003. Toggle growth and reset to compare against the original CAVE-001 control. Crowded particles grow, recolor, and change bond rest lengths; sparse particles recover.

Click/tap the simulation to apply a radial impulse. Seeded initial conditions are replayable on reset; manually applied impulses are not replayed.

OBSERVATORY

The universe begins at its first tick.

Particles attract, repel at short distances, and form/break spring bonds. Stable connected structures emerge if the rules permit them.

Toy physics, not a cosmological model. Fixed-step seeded simulation; reset repeats the same initial conditions.

THE FIRST ROOM

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or controller. Interact using E, Space, or controller A.

Activate the glowing points to form a room. Find the doorway to discover another chamber.

Rule Map

Points connect into lines. Closing a loop forms a room. Opening a room creates a corridor. Crossing a corridor reveals another chamber.

Future generations may change these rules, while preserving this playable ancestor.
